✦ ABOUT US ✦

The Path of Dharma

SWC isn't just a club — it's a movement. We bring ancient dharmic wisdom into modern student life to cultivate intellect, emotional balance, and spiritual growth.

Our Mission

In today's fast-paced academic environment, thousands of students lose their way to anxiety, stress, and lack of purpose. The Student Welfare Club exists to provide an anchor.

Through the timeless teachings of the Bhagavad Gita, we intend to forge spiritually grounded leaders who execute their duties (Karma) with unparalleled focus and surrender the results to the divine.

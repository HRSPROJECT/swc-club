✦ OUR TEACHINGS ✦

The Four Paths.
One Eternal Truth.

Lord Krishna revealed multiple paths to liberation in the Bhagavad Gita.
At SWC, we study and practice each to attain highest mastery.

PATH OF ACTION

Karma Yoga

"You have the right to perform your prescribed duty, but you are not entitled to the fruits of action."

Karma Yoga is the secret of work. It teaches us to perform our duties with utmost dedication, efficiency, and zero attachment to the final reward. By surrendering the outcome to the divine, the mind remains unbothered by success or failure.

PATH OF DEVOTION

Bhakti Yoga

"Engage your mind always in thinking of Me, become My devotee, offer obeisances to Me..."

Bhakti Yoga is considered the most supreme and accessible path. Through pure, unconditional love and devotion to God, the ego dissolves. Chanting, singing bhajans, and feeling constant gratitude are integral practices of Bhakti.

PATH OF MEDITATION

Dhyana (Raja) Yoga

"For him who has conquered the mind, the mind is the best of friends..."

Total mastery over the restless mind requires rigorous discipline, pranayama, and sustained dhyana (meditation). SWC conducts silent retreats where students disconnect from the world to map the inner universe.

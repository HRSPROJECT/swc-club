Words That Echo
Across Eternity

Immerse yourself in the eternal verses of the Bhagavad Gita.

ॐ
Chapter 4 · Verse 8
परित्राणाय साधूनां विनाशाय च दुष्कृताम्।
धर्मसंस्थापनार्थाय सम्भवामि युगे युगे॥
"For the protection of the righteous, for the destruction of the wicked, and for the establishment of dharma, I am born in every age."
— Lord Krishna
Chapter 2 · Verse 20
न जायते म्रियते वा कदाचिन्नायं भूत्वा भविता वा न भूयः।
अजो नित्यः शाश्वतोऽयं पुराणो न हन्यते हन्यमाने शरीरे॥
"The soul is never born nor dies at any time. It has not come into being, does not come into being, and will not come into being."
— Lord Krishna
